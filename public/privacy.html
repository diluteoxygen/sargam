Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to Sargam! We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you play our game.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Sargam, we may collect:

  • Account Information: If you sign in (e.g., via Google), we securely receive your basic profile information (name, email, and profile picture) provided by the authentication provider.
  • Gameplay Data: We store your game stats, scores, streaks, and unlocked achievements so you can track your progress.
  • Analytics: We collect aggregated, anonymous usage data (like which songs are played or skipped) to improve the game's difficulty and song selection.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Save your gameplay progress across devices.
  • Maintain global and daily leaderboards.
  • Identify bugs and improve the overall user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services like Firebase (for authentication, database, and hosting). These services may collect information sent by your browser as part of a web page request, such as cookies or your IP address, subject to their own privacy policies.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption provided by Google Firebase. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access or delete your personal data. If you wish to delete your Sargam account and wipe your gameplay history, you can contact us or use the account deletion tool in the app (if available).

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@sargam.com.
